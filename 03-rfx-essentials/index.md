# RFx Essentials (RFI / RFQ / RFP)

This section covers the formal and informal ways deals actually enter your pipeline — from a structured **RFI/RFQ/RFP** process run by a procurement team to a casual "can you send us something" from a champion who has no idea what a scoping document even is. You'll learn how to tell the three formal request types apart, when each one signals real buying intent versus vendor-list building, and how to read a client document for the unstated priorities buried in its requirements. By the end you'll know how to shape a response — formal or informal — that actually gets read and wins, instead of getting lost in a stack of boilerplate submissions.
