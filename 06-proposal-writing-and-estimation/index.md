# Proposal Writing & Estimation

This section covers how to turn discovery findings into a proposal that wins: structuring the document so it argues the client's problem before it pitches your solution, defining scope, deliverables, assumptions, and risks tightly enough to prevent disputes later, choosing an estimation method that fits how well the work is actually understood, and picking a pricing model that puts risk where it belongs. Get this wrong and even a strong solution loses on a vague scope statement, a padded or unexplained number, or a pricing model mismatched to the client's risk appetite. By the end of this section you should be able to build a proposal that reads as a logical, defensible argument from problem to price.
