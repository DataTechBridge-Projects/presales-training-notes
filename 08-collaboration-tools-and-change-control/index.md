# Collaboration, Tools, and Change Control

A deal doesn't end when the champion says yes — it moves into a handoff between the people who sold it and the people who have to build it, and that handoff is where good pre-sales work quietly falls apart if nobody owns it. This section covers how to stay useful across that boundary: keeping the account executive, solutions engineers, and delivery leads aligned on what was actually promised, and keeping a clean, defensible record when scope shifts after the ink is dry. Get this wrong and you inherit disputes, re-work, and a reputation for over-promising; get it right and you become the person both sides trust to keep the deal honest.
