# Extra Section: AI for Pre-Sales

This section shifts focus from process to tooling: how AI actually fits into a pre-sales workflow, and how to scope AI-related opportunities for clients with real credibility. You'll see the technique end to end — including a full walkthrough of turning a client's RFP into a clickable prototype — and build the vocabulary and frameworks to identify, position, and estimate AI-driven work across predictive, generative, and agentic AI. It closes with a practical lens for weighing any AI initiative's business value against its cost and risk, so you can guide a client toward the right scope instead of chasing whatever's trending.
