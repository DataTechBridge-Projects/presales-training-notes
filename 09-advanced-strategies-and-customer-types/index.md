# Advanced Strategies & Customer Types

This section moves past the mechanics of RFx, discovery, and contracts into the judgment calls that separate a competent pre-sales rep from a trusted one. You'll build a reusable toolkit that speeds up routine work, sharpen the specific language that keeps a SOW protective without making it rigid, and develop tactics for the stakeholders who don't follow the tidy process you'd prefer. It also covers two situations that don't fit the standard direct-sales playbook — deals that arrive through a cloud partner's program, and moments where the client's actual need is bigger than what they asked for — both of which reward a rep who can operate outside the default script.
