# Extra Section: Business Trips

Client-facing travel is still a routine part of pre-sales work — on-site discovery visits, demo days, executive briefings, and partner events all happen in person when the deal is big enough or the relationship is new enough to warrant it. This section covers how to plan and run a business trip so the travel itself never becomes the reason a deal stalls: aligning with the account team before you go, building in a backup plan for the inevitable demo or connectivity failure, and following up fast enough that the trip actually moves the opportunity forward. Treat it as the field-logistics counterpart to the discovery and demo material covered elsewhere in the course.
