# Extra Section: Azure Partner Services

This section covers the funding levers Microsoft makes available to partners selling and delivering Azure work — money that can turn a stalled "let's think about it" into a funded assessment or pilot. It walks through **ECIF**, **AMM**, and **Azure Innovate**: what each one actually pays for, the paperwork and approvals each requires, and the point in a deal where invoking one makes sense. If you've worked through the Google or AWS partner funding sections, the underlying logic is familiar — vendor-subsidized proof points that lower a customer's risk of committing — but the specific programs, thresholds, and triggers here are Microsoft's own.
