# Introduction to Pre-Sales

This opening section sets the stage before you touch a single RFx or contract: what pre-sales actually is, how this guide is organized, and who's walking you through it. It's short by design, but skipping it means missing the map you'll want when later sections start referencing discovery calls, SOWs, and estimation frameworks by name. Treat it as the five minutes that make the next forty hours make sense.
