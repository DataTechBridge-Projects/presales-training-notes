# Extra Section: Google Partner Services

This section covers Google's partner-funded programs — the Partner Sales Fund (PSF) and Partner Solutions Organization (PSO) — and how to use them as a pre-sales lever rather than an afterthought bolted onto a proposal. You'll learn how to apply for funding, what Google actually approves versus what gets rejected, and how PSF and PSO differ in scope and timing across the deal lifecycle. The closing lecture walks through composite case studies showing concretely how this funding changes deal economics on real GCP engagements.
