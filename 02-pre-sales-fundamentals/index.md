# Pre-Sales Fundamentals

This section lays the groundwork the rest of the guide builds on: what pre-sales actually is, the stages an opportunity moves through from qualification to closed deal, and who owns what along the way. Every later section — RFx handling, discovery calls, contract documents, proposals — assumes you already know these stage names and role boundaries, so it's worth reading closely even though it's short. Think of it as the shared vocabulary that lets an account executive, a solutions architect, and a delivery lead all describe the same deal the same way.
