# Discovery & Requirements Gathering

This section is where a pre-sales engagement stops being a pitch and starts being a diagnosis. You'll learn why discovery — not the demo, not the proposal — is where deals are actually won or lost, and pick up a repeatable four-step framework, **Pain → Impact → Value → Fit**, for running that conversation with any stakeholder. Two role-play scenarios then let you hear the framework in action: first in a straightforward discovery call, then in a harder conversation with a skeptical executive pushing back on price and priority before you've finished asking questions.
