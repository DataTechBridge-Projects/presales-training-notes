# Extra Section: AWS Partner Services

This section covers the AWS partner funding programs you'll draw on most often when a deal needs a financial nudge to close or de-risk. AWS puts real money behind partners who bring qualified opportunities to the table — proof-of-concept credits, migration funding, modernization incentives, and generative AI-specific programs — and knowing which one fits a given deal shape can shrink a customer's perceived risk and shorten your sales cycle. This matters in pre-sales because these programs are usually invoked during scoping and proposal, not after the contract is signed, so you need to recognize the trigger conditions early enough to actually use them.
